Support

For the iPhone and iPad games Ring Sort, Bluff Code (الخزنة الكدّابة), Light Lines, Drop (طيّحها), Nujaima (نُجَيمة) and Tisa (تِسعة).

Contact: aalem1922@icloud.com
Please say which game, your device and iOS version. Replies usually within a few days.

Hints didn't arrive after a purchase

Close the game completely and open it again while connected to the internet. The game picks up any purchase Apple confirmed and adds the hints once. If they still don't appear, email us with the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress on a new device

Stars, progress and hints are kept in your own iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud, and they come back after you reinstall or move to a new device.

Leaderboards and achievements

These need Game Center. Sign in from Settings → Game Center on your device.

المشتريات تتم عن طريق Apple، وتقدر تطلب الاسترجاع من reportaproblem.apple.com.
